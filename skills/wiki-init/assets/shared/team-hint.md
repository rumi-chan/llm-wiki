## Project wiki

This repo has a shared wiki in `docs/wiki/`, maintained mostly by AI agents.

- At the start of a session, read `docs/wiki/SCHEMA.md` and `docs/wiki/index.md`, and follow the schema.
- At the end of a task that changes documented behaviour, update the affected pages in the same
  branch, as described in the schema ("Update for your change"). Minimal edits.
- Never edit `docs/wiki/index.md` or `docs/wiki/.sync-state` by hand.
